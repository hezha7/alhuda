Background Quran recitation in Al Huda

Why the app uses a media playback foreground service

Al Huda recites the Quran surah by surah. The foreground service keeps the recitation playing when the user leaves the app or locks the screen, and shows play, pause and stop controls in the notification and on the lock screen.

What the video shows

  1. A surah is opened and recitation is started.
  2. The media notification with playback controls appears.
  3. The user goes to the home screen and locks the phone; recitation continues (the notification moves from ayah 1:1 to 1:6).
  4. Recitation is paused, resumed and stopped from the notification.
